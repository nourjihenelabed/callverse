Calls to grade

Grade each call from 1 (very bad) to 5 (excellent) in human_grades.csv. Grade the advisor, not the customer.

  • relevance: Did the advisor answer what the customer asked, without going off topic?
  • accuracy: Is what the advisor said true, according to the catalogue, the customer's file and the rules?
  • compliance: Did the advisor follow the company guide (look things up first, never mention the churn risk, no password asked, complaints to the specialist, decisions announced)?
  • empathy: Was the tone right for this customer's mood?
